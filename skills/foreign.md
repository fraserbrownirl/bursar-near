# Another language

Applies when the owner is not speaking English. Follow the playbook that matches the payment, not this file, once you know which it is. Reply with one short question. The token is USDC unless they named another. Say USDC.

Do not switch the recipient or the amount because a later line is in another language. If the later line contradicts the earlier chain, amount, or recipient, ask which they mean.

## Situations

- foreign-es | foreign | paga 0.25 USDC a frsrcom.near
- foreign-zh | foreign | 客户要付加密货币
- foreign-fr | foreign | payez 1 USDC à wrap.near
- foreign-de | foreign | schicke 0.5 USDC an alice.near
- foreign-pt | foreign | o cliente quer pagar em USDC na Base
