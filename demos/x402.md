# Paid API (x402)

## What it is

The owner asks Bursar to find a weather API, then to pay for one call. Bursar searches the x402 Bazaar, picks a payable service, reads its 402 price, shows the card, and pays the exact amount on Base. The API answers with the weather. x402 is the HTTP status code 402 turned into a payment protocol: the service's response says the price, the asset, and the address; the payment goes in the retry's header.

## What's real

- The money: 0.001 USDC on Base mainnet, from the Base float (Keychain item `bursar-evm`, address `0xE7369dD4F08fA5203B06F3E1Dccee00a11cCF152`) to the service's `0xd6fCE15298Fc9D60f867d9455D497177Da9BFB0a`, scheme `exact`, network `eip155:8453`. Settled in https://basescan.org/tx/0x50c5acc7a2de4fbcccde1fd4ab77ca5c6bd1bed2eb5f03ab9a18eff1529cbb70 .
- The discovery: `bursar find weather` searched the Bazaar and marked this service payable (`docs/hackathon/evidence.json`, the bazaar run of 2026-11-11: `https://x402.tbs-digital.com/weather/current`, 1000 atomic USDC, scheme `exact`).
- The guards: the card and the 1 USDC cap apply to this payment too (`src/x402-pay.ts`).

## What's staged

- The service is a third-party test endpoint, and the amount is a tenth of a cent. There are no in-model receipts for this scene: the quote check and the intent check belong to the NEAR Intents flow; a Base `exact` payment is signed by the float key, not by the guarded Intents signer.

## How it's proven

- The settlement is a public Base transaction; the link opens without any account.
- `docs/hackathon/evidence.json` records the payment: amount `1000` (0.001 USDC), `payTo` `0xd6fCE15298Fc9D60f867d9455D497177Da9BFB0a`, request id `ec4deaaa-5bb0-430d-88a8-90f2a4d5731c`, and the transaction hash above.
- A judge repeats it the way the owner did: `bursar find weather`, then `pay <url>` for a service the Bazaar marks payable, funded by their own float.

## Run on 2026-11-12

The owner typed `find me a weather API`, then `pay for one call`. The service answered the 402 with its price (0.001 USDC on Base), the card showed that price, and after approval the weather for London came back with the settlement link. The command form of the same flow: `npm run bursar -- pay 'https://x402.tbs-digital.com/weather/current?city=London'`.
